== 2.2.0 ==
Advanced FanFicFare options are checked when you save them: a line FanFicFare cannot read is refused with its line number, and a username, password or cookie line is refused with a link to Settings → Credentials, where sign-ins belong. Confirm adult content now wins over is_adult in Advanced FanFicFare options, for every site; saving such a line shows a warning. Needs ebookerr 2.24.14 or later.

== 2.1.0 ==
A chapter row records the story it belongs to as its story, no longer as a series; a whole story keeps the series its site declares. Needs ebookerr 2.24.7.

== 2.0.1 ==
Uses FanFicFare 4.62.0, the version this release was tested with; newer FanFicFare releases arrive as updates of this plugin a few days after they are published.

== 2.0.0 ==
Has its own settings in Settings → Plugins — Confirm adult content and Advanced FanFicFare options — and uses the sign-in stored in Settings → Credentials for each page's site, instead of reading the FanFicFare plugin's personal.ini: before updating, copy anything you changed there into Advanced FanFicFare options. Its card shows that it uses your site sign-ins. Updated for ebookerr's plugin interface 3.0. Needs ebookerr 2.24 or later.

== 1.3.0 ==
Story extraction no longer fills the ebookerr log with FanFicFare's debug output or a false warning, and a slow listing page still gives up after one retry. Needs FanFicFare 4.62 or later.

== 1.2.0 ==
Declares its story-extraction role in the new manifest format. Needs ebookerr 2.21 or later.

== 1.1.0 ==
Moved out of the ebookerr core.
