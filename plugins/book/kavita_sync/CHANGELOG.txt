== 1.9.0 ==
A reading position read back from Kavita now carries the time you were there, not the time ebookerr noticed it, so reading done while ebookerr was closed keeps its order and its day. Marking a book unread in Kavita reaches ebookerr within a minute while a page is open. A book whose chapters Kavita cannot match is reported in the log once instead of every minute. Needs ebookerr 2.24.11 or later.

== 1.8.0 ==
A book whose title or series you change in ebookerr stays linked to Kavita: when Kavita files it under a new chapter, the next sync finds it by its file and moves the link, and a link already lost this way comes back at the next sync. Needs ebookerr 2.24.1 or later.

== 1.7.0 ==
Its icon is now a library, shown on its card and on its Sync to Kavita button. Needs ebookerr 2.24.1 or later.

== 1.6.0 ==
Updated for ebookerr's plugin interface 3.0; it works as before. Needs ebookerr 2.24 or later.

== 1.5.0 ==
ebookerr's own web reader and KOReader sync can now save your reading position to Kavita: it lands on the chapter you are in, at the page of that chapter nearest your place. Needs ebookerr 2.23 or later.

== 1.4.0 ==
Reading progress you make in Kavita now reaches ebookerr within about a minute while an ebookerr page is open, and at once when you open the book's page in ebookerr. Every read-back carries Kavita's own timestamp, so an older report never overwrites a newer reading position. Needs ebookerr 2.22 or later.

== 1.3.0 ==
Declares its library-server role in the new manifest format. Needs ebookerr 2.21 or later.

== 1.2.0 ==
Reading positions follow the book's own finished state: a finished book that gains a chapter keeps your place and is no longer shown as read. Restoring a position that is already where your reader stands no longer rewrites it. Backward-move warnings and first-link backups are now handled by ebookerr itself, and a bookmark re-anchored on weak evidence now tells you so.

== 1.1.0 ==
Moved out of the ebookerr core.
