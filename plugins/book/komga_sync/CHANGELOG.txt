== 1.12.0 ==
A book whose chapters Komga has not re-read yet is reported once, not at every sync. Books merged before 2.24.17 now keep their reading position in Komga. When Komga already holds a newer position from another app, ebookerr keeps it quietly. Needs ebookerr 2.24.17 or later.

== 1.11.0 ==
A place you saved in a chapter is no longer read back as an earlier place, so you no longer get a false "moved back" warning after reading in ebookerr or KOReader. Needs ebookerr 2.24.13 or later.

== 1.10.0 ==
A reading position read back from Komga now carries the time you were there, not the time ebookerr noticed it, so reading done while ebookerr was closed keeps its order and its day. Marking a book unread in Komga reaches ebookerr within a minute while a page is open, for the 100 books read most recently. A book whose chapters Komga cannot match is reported in the log once instead of every minute. Needs ebookerr 2.24.11 or later.

== 1.9.0 ==
A book with several authors is now found in Komga by its authors in any order, so its link no longer breaks when Komga lists them differently, and its authors are read with ebookerr's own rule for "&". Needs ebookerr 2.24.10 or later.

== 1.8.0 ==
Changing a book's category no longer leaves the old category behind as a tag after the next sync, and a tag you remove in ebookerr is no longer brought back from Komga. Tags added earlier this way stay until you remove them. Needs ebookerr 2.24.1 or later.

== 1.7.0 ==
Its Sync to Komga button now shows the plugin's own icon. Needs ebookerr 2.24.1 or later.

== 1.6.0 ==
Updated for ebookerr's plugin interface 3.0; it works as before. Needs ebookerr 2.24 or later.

== 1.5.0 ==
ebookerr's own web reader and KOReader sync can now save your reading position to Komga: it lands on the chapter you are in, at the nearest position inside it. Needs ebookerr 2.23 or later.

== 1.4.0 ==
Reading progress you make in Komga now reaches ebookerr within about a minute while an ebookerr page is open, and at once when you open the book's page in ebookerr. Every author, the series number, a valid ISBN (an ISBN-10 is converted) and the publisher are now sent to Komga. Every read-back carries Komga's own timestamp, so an older report never overwrites a newer reading position. Needs ebookerr 2.22 or later.

== 1.3.0 ==
Declares its library-server role in the new manifest format. Needs ebookerr 2.21 or later.

== 1.2.0 ==
Reading positions follow the book's own finished state: a finished book that gains a chapter keeps your place and is no longer shown as read. Restoring a position that is already where your reader stands no longer rewrites it. Backward-move warnings and first-link backups are now handled by ebookerr itself, and a bookmark re-anchored on weak evidence now tells you so.

== 1.1.0 ==
Moved out of the ebookerr core.
